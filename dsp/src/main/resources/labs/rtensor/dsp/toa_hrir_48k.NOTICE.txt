toa_hrir_48k.f32: third-order ambisonic (ACN channel order, SN3D) left-ear
head-related impulse responses, 16 x 256 float32 little-endian at 48 kHz.

Extracted unchanged (16-bit PCM converted to float) from Omnitone 2.0.0
(OmnitoneTOAHrirBase64), Copyright 2016 Google Inc., Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/GoogleChrome/omnitone

Omnitone's filters are derived from the SADIE binaural database,
University of York: https://www.york.ac.uk/sadie-project/
